When a responder goes quiet, someone notices and there is a way back

The brief for Helen, mapped to this prototype.

Who it's for

A responder who has gone quiet, and the handler who looks after them. Meteor Mite and Kip are the example on the Responders tab. Farlight went from about 12 pings a week to none by 24 Aug.

What changes for them

Responder: offered callouts again; a fresh start isn't judged on the old record.
Handler: a banner and "Gone quiet" status.
Misses fade: use the decay slider on a quiet responder.
Callouts: go back to the usual covering responder.

What it deliberately does not do

No revert of 4.2 or change to proximity. No change to the ping wait. No single-number tweak. No guaranteed share of callouts. No identifying anyone: cover names only. No change to how availability is calculated (Supply reads it). No mutual aid. Doesn't answer whether the ranking change was meant to apply to responders who turn jobs down (Wen Li).

Timeline

This week: weekly alert.
Next release (~4.3): handler view with fresh start, and decay.
Within a quarter: miss counts less than a refusal; possible minimum pings.
Later, with evidence: revisit the ping wait; write down how ping decisions are made.

Not yet checked

Wen Li hasn't confirmed the miss penalty, stored scores, or whether scores reset on restart. Nobody has verified Farlight's (or Meteor Mite's) availability record. No per-ping answer times. The handler evidence is thin: 4 interviews, no responders. Data ends 6 Sep. The decay slider and the five-ping simulation are illustrations, not real routing.
